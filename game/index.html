YOU WIN THE PACT

How to Play

The duel. You and Rook each have 20 life. Every round you both pick one card and reveal it at the same time. First to 0 life loses; if both fall in the same round it is a draw.

The deck. 40 cards: four suits, values 1–10. You start with 5 cards and draw 1 each round (hand cap 7). If the deck runs out, hands play out; with both hands empty, higher life wins.

Momentum. Play the same suit as your previous round and the card gains +2 before its effect.

  • ▲ Flame — Attack. Deal power damage; shield absorbs it first.
  • ● Tide — Guard. Gain power shield (max 12).
  • ◆ Thorn — Leech. Deal half power, piercing shield, and heal the same.
  • ■ Stone — Quarry. Gain half power shield and draw a card.

Rook. The rival thinks out loud in the log: it guards when hurt, leeches through thick shields, and chases its own momentum. Read it and counter it.

Controls. Click a card to select it, click Play (or double-click the card). Keys: 1–7 select · Enter/Space play · N new game · H help · Esc title.
